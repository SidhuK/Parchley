# Code of conduct

Parchley contributors are expected to communicate with respect, assume good
faith while investigating problems, and focus criticism on the work rather than
the person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private
information, and disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit,
decline, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.

Report a conduct concern privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do
not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain the
report.
